A mobile editor should preserve the work inside it. **0.28.0-preview.1** adds optional `/bottom-sheet` and `/bottom-sheet.css`: three considered resting heights around an authored native dialog.

Native form controls, autofocus and scrolling stay intact. Tap/Enter/Space resize; the dedicated grabber handles dragging. A guarded dismissal can keep unsaved edits until the host approves closing. There is one pending decision per unchanged edit state, and old approvals become stale after reopening or native input.

[Field / Notes](https://wpsydev.github.io/liquid-glass-kit/sheet/) has two original local briefs, real page-session save, Keep editing and Discard. Its finite11.2-second tour shows medium, writing and large heights. An original twelve-second film with a124BPM afro/marimba score is attached.
