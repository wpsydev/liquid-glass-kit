# Soft Matter film

The 0.11 film records the production-source `/fusion/` studio in native T3 Chrome 152 on Apple M1 Pro. Three decorative droplets merge into a shared silhouette, orbit and separate across cobalt, citrus and rose light. Analytic surface normals, edge refraction, RGB dispersion and moving calibration stripes make the material visible. This is an independent optical experiment, not Apple's compositor.

The twelve-second sequence includes an end card. Native HTML controls remain visible outside the decorative canvas; only the drag hint is hidden during the sequence. The gallery's 900,000-pixel budget produced an 807×1114 backing buffer at the recorded 720×1280 viewport. It uses three scene draws: background, presentation and one bounded fusion pass. The film is a presentation artifact, not a performance measurement.

Export: `assets/fusion-showcase.mp4`, 720×1280 H.264/yuv420p, 30 export fps, 360 frames, twelve seconds, stereo 48 kHz AAC, fast-start MP4. Only a 0.20-second leading trim and normal frame resampling are applied. Music is the original 160 BPM score generated by `scripts/render-fusion-beat.py`, without external samples, under the repository's MIT license.

Full video/audio decoding and six contact frames passed review, including the end card. Decoded audio mean is −14.5 dBFS and peak −2.1 dBFS. See `fusion-update.json` for source hashes, responsive layouts, context recovery, reduced-motion fixture, export verification and publication receipts. The controlled GPU workload and its tail timings are documented separately in [the material guide](../FUSION.md).
