A small policy, not a benchmark

Evidence has a source.
Quality has a cost.

Pressure needs a mean above 1.15× the timing budget and at least 75% of samples above that threshold. Recovery needs a mean below 0.65× and at least 90% below it, sustained for more windows.

Rejected measurements, gaps and cooldowns clear adaptation evidence. A manual lock keeps the requested level fixed. Frame cadence cannot establish GPU cost or prove that fewer canvas pixels will make a page faster.

The native run starts a fresh governor with production defaults. The fixture laboratory uses shorter, clearly reported windows so you can inspect every branch. Neither is a speedup claim.
