The 44px target and 16px field checks are library policies. WCAG 2.2’s target-size minimum is 24px with exceptions that this tool does not evaluate. A measurable border box is not proof of a complete clickable shape or a working keyboard flow.

Labels and intentional horizontal scrolling are declared by this host. Hidden and clipped controls are skipped; offscreen geometry remains distinct. A truncated or unavailable measurement is incomplete, not a clean mobile verdict.

These explicit synchronous calls can force style or layout work. Read counts are sampler operations, not browser paint cost, GPU timings or app FPS. Check physical devices, Safari, assistive technology and real interaction flows separately.
