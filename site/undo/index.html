Under the surface

A real receipt.
A real compensation.

Apply returns a receipt after the local object moves. Undo uses that same receipt to restore the original object. Commit resolves after the local archive is finalized.

Timed mode is explicit. Focus, pointer, manual, and hidden-page pauses compose. The remaining-time display is sampled at events, not polled or presented as a continuously ticking clock.

Rejected callbacks do not invent recovery. The delayed Apply fixture ignores abort; a disposed model can report a stale receipt while its actual host change still needs history Restore.
